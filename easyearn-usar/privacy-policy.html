Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

EasyEarn is operated by MD RACHEL SHK NOYON. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the EasyEarn app and its connected services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, phone number, account identifier, and optional profile picture or referral code.
  • Authentication: the password you enter is processed by Firebase Authentication. The application database does not store your password as plain text. An internal phone-based email alias is initially used for sign-in. If you add and verify a Recovery Email, Firebase Authentication uses that real email for authentication, verification and password-reset messages. Your phone number remains your EasyEarn login ID.
  • Transactions: add-balance and withdrawal amounts, selected payment method, sender or receiving account number, transaction reference, request status, balance, and request/decision dates.
  • Activity: quiz answers and progress, rewards, transaction history, last-active time, and notification read status.
  • Device identifiers: Firebase installation/authentication identifiers and notification registration tokens, linked to your account to deliver your own updates.
  • Technical information: our hosting and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request metadata, and security/operational logs when your device connects.
  • Support: information you choose to send by email.

2. Why we use this information

We use it to create and secure accounts, display balances and history, review payment requests, calculate quiz rewards, prevent duplicate or fraudulent transactions, provide support, and deliver general announcements or account-specific notifications.

3. Notifications and your choices

Notifications are optional. You can allow or disable them in your phone's app settings. Notification tokens are managed automatically and are not displayed in the user interface. Transaction notifications can contain financial amounts and may appear on your lock screen; you can hide sensitive notification content in your phone settings.

4. Who processes or receives information

Authorized EasyEarn administrators can access account and transaction information needed to run the service. We use Google Firebase for authentication, database storage and messaging; Cloudflare for backend processing and notification delivery records; and GitHub Pages for hosting the app's web content. Content resources may be loaded from Google Fonts and public asset/CDN providers, which receive normal network request information. Support emails are handled through Gmail.

Payment account details are used by authorized administrators to verify or arrange payments through the selected payment service. External websites opened from the app have their own privacy policies. We may disclose information when required by law or to investigate abuse. We do not sell personal information.

5. Storage and security

Connections use HTTPS. Authentication and database access controls restrict users to their own private records. Service providers may process data outside Bangladesh. No online service can guarantee complete security.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account and activity records while your account is active and needed to provide the service. To request account and associated data deletion, email onlinesuppershop24hours@gmail.com with the subject EasyEarn Account Deletion and your registered phone number. Do not send your password. We verify ownership before deleting an account.

Deletion requests cover your authentication account, profile, profile picture, account activity and linked notification records. Transaction or security records may be retained only where needed to resolve pending transactions/disputes, prevent fraud, or meet applicable legal obligations; we will explain any such retention when handling your request. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

You can also submit an authenticated request under Profile → Delete Account, or use our account deletion request page.

7. Access, corrections and account control

You can view your profile and history and change your password in the app. Contact us to request corrections, access to your information, or deletion. Notification preferences can be changed in Android settings.

8. Children

EasyEarn's money-related account service is intended for adults aged 18 or older. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it.

9. Policy changes

We may update this policy when our service or data practices change. The updated date will appear on this page. Material changes will be communicated through the app where appropriate.
