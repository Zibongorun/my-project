EasyEarn Account Deletion

Developer: MD RACHEL SHK NOYON

You can request deletion without installing or logging into the app.

How to request deletion

Email onlinesuppershop24hours@gmail.com with the subject EasyEarn Account Deletion and your registered phone number. Do not send your password or payment PIN.

This button opens your email app. Send the email there to submit the request. If no email app opens, email the address above directly.

In the app, you can instead open Profile → Delete Account, verify your password and submit a request.

What happens next

Support verifies account ownership, reviews your remaining balance and pending transactions, and processes the deletion. Requests are reviewed manually. We will explain any records that need to be retained when handling your request.

Data covered

The request covers your Firebase sign-in account, name, phone-linked profile, profile picture, activity/quiz history and linked notification identifiers. Financial or security records may be retained only when needed for unresolved transactions, disputes, fraud prevention or applicable legal obligations. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Read Privacy Policy

Completed deletion removes your authentication account, profile, activity, linked notification records and settled in-app transaction records. A restricted completion receipt containing only the account identifier, completion status and time is kept for up to 30 days for retry handling, then removed.
